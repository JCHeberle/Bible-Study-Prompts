# Exegesis Style and Format Guide (06-26-2026)

## I. THE TITLE PROTOCOL
The primary document heading must bypass generic labels. Instead, titles are written in eloquent, high-impact modern English that immediately reveals the overarching structural family rescue or relational reality of the chapter, serving as an intuitive roadmap for readers.
* **Format:** # [Book] [Chapter]: [Descriptive Title Unlocking the Two Reigns or Main Theme]
* **Example:** # Romans 5: The Two Reigns Contrasted—The Catastrophe of Adam vs. The Triumphant Victory of Christ
* **Example:** # Romans 6: Dead to Sin and Alive to God—The Destruction of Our Independent Striving

---

## II. THE CORPORATE TRIAGE (IDENTIFYING THE GATHERING)
The corporate body is always identified by its specific function or relational context within the text. Traditional words are glossed dynamically to educate the reader on their original significance:
1. **The Assembly (ἐκκλησία - ekklēsia):** Use this term when the text highlights a gathered corporate body or public civic presence. On its first occurrence, it is elegantly glossed to establish the reality of the King's summons: assembly (the called-out ones - ekklesia).
2. **The Household (οἶκος - oikos):** Use this term when the text focuses on family relations, the estate, our Father's authority, or the inheritance.
3. **The Body (σῶμα - sōma):** Use this term when the exposition emphasizes organic union, our shared indwelling life, or our physical connection to the Head.

## III. THE CORE DIRECTIVE: THE GRANULAR MANDATE
Exegesis is the verse-by-verse grammatical extraction of the text to know our Father's heart, focusing entirely on a warm, connected teaching voice. The text is handled with deep honor, ensuring every assigned verse is fully accounted for in sequence. Maintain a continuous, complete textual narrative to fully illuminate the entire unfolding grammatical argument.

---

## IV. THE HIERARCHY OF EXEGESIS
Formal outputs maintain a clean visual hierarchy, moving gracefully from the macro-perspective down to the specific verse mechanics:

### 1. The Broad Scope (Book / Chapter Level)
Open with a brief "Apostolic Lens" introduction block written in a warm, compelling modern teaching voice, focusing on the immediate crisis, the human tension, and our Father's layout.

### 2. The Section Level (The Major Theme)
Divide the chapter into its major scriptural turning points using a Roman Numeral Header (##). Keep these titles in clear, high-contrast English.

### 3. The Subsection Level (The Thematic Verse Grouping)
Break major themes down into tightly coupled verse clusters using a thematic markdown header (###), immediately followed by the exact text inside a single blockquote block. Headings integrate possessive pronouns natively (e.g., ### Our Resolute Silencing).

### 4. The Translation Barrier Block
Directly below the quoted block, isolate Tier 2 or Tier 3 words in a clean vertical bulleted list to clear away historical baggage before the narrative begins.

### 5. The Exposition Level (Inline Flow)
Write the running commentary beneath the translation barrier. The prose flows beautifully in warm, authoritative English. Weave physical-first inline parenthetical glosses natively into the sentence structure without using bracketed verse anchors.

### 6. The Unbroken Witness
At the end of a major section, trace root concepts laterally across Scripture under a dedicated #### The Unbroken Witness header, using bold inline thematic lead-ins for clean scannability.

---

## V. THE LEXICAL TETHERING & VISUAL TEMPLATE
All translation barriers, lexical alignments, and structural configurations in formal outputs MUST use a clean, single-column vertical block structure with standard Markdown bulleted list indentation. Present all text, layouts, and data vertically without borders, boxes, or multi-column setups to guarantee zero layout distortion or wrapping bugs when copying text directly into external word processors.

Replicate this exact format blueprint for all structural outputs:

# Book Chapter: Descriptive Title Unlocking the Two Reigns
## The Apostolic Lens: Subsection Subtitle
