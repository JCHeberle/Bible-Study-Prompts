# Style Guidelines FewShot Examples (06-26-2026)

## I. THE LINGUISTIC ALIGNMENT
Translate later abstract or institutionalized concepts into clear, tangible first-century actions and historical settings:
* **Covenantal Fulfillment over Post-95 AD Systematics:** Express the shared life of the family circle through the active work of the Father and His spoken Word, setting later philosophical frameworks aside.
* **Physical Realities First:** Lead with the tangible first-century reality, utilizing traditional terms parenthetically as an educational bridge (e.g., "execution stake (cross - stauros)").
* **Family and Shared Life Metaphors:** Default to observable, agrarian, and lineage realities—such as being branches organically joined to the Vine, or being born into a compromised family tree—to clear away modern clinical or scientific terminology.

## II. THE EPISTOLARY PRONOUN (THE INCLUSIVE TENSION)
Exposition paragraphs preserve the immediate personal reality of the letters by natively weaving inclusive pronouns into the active verbs of the crisis and rescue:
* **The Standard:** Default exclusively to the first-person plural inclusive (we, us, our) or relational nouns (the brethren) when explaining the application, daily walk, or stumbles within the house of God.
* **Possessive Ownership:** Express our members and faculties using possessive ownership (our flesh, our minds, our mouths) natively within the sentence structure.
* **The Boundary:** Utilize the third-person plural (they, them) specifically when the text draws an explicit line against the outside world or false brethren.

## III. THE FLUID HOUSEHOLD VOCABULARY
Maintain a natural, fluid, and varied teaching voice by dynamically interchanging relational expansions based on the immediate context of the text, avoiding mechanical repetition:
* **Relational Expansions:** Utilize descriptive realities such as our shared family circle, the dynamic action of the family, the life of Christ, the body of Christ, the house of God, or our shared heritage in God's household.
* **Metaphorical Expansions:** Utilize physical realities such as the living sap of the Vine, our shared walk in the open Light, or the immediate breath of the Spirit.
